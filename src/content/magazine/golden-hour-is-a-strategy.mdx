Scroll through the feeds of the brands you admire and you will notice something
they rarely say out loud: the light is always the same. Not the location, not
the product, not even the faces — the light. That consistency is not an
accident, and it is not a preset. It is a decision.

## Light is the cheapest brand asset you are not using

Most brands invest in logos, palettes and typefaces, then shoot their content
under whatever light the day offers. The result is a feed that feels like six
different companies. Committing to a lighting character — for us, the warm,
low, directional light of the last two hours of sun — does the work of a brand
guideline without printing one.

A few things happen when the light becomes a rule:

- Every new post looks like it belongs to the ones before it.
- Products photographed months apart sit together in campaigns without regrading.
- Followers recognize the brand before they read the handle.

## Consistency beats intensity

> A feed that whispers the same thing every day outperforms one that shouts
> something different every week.

The temptation is to chase variety — new looks, new grades, new moods. But
recognition compounds, and recognition is built on repetition. Choose the
light, write it down, and hold every shoot to it: golden hour on location,
tungsten-warmed setups in studio, the same white balance discipline in edit.

## Where to start

Audit your last thirty posts and group them by light temperature. If you find
more than two groups, you have found the reason the feed feels scattered. Pick
the group that best matches how the brand should *feel* — then make it law.
